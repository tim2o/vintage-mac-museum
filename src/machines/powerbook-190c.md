The badge on this machine reads PowerBook 190cs, but its screen has been swapped with one from a PowerBook 5300c. That technically makes it a 190c, a model that never existed. According to EveryMac, the 190cs shipped with a passive-matrix display and the 5300c with an active-matrix one. Apple System Profiler reports a 68LC040 at 33 MHz with 32 MB of memory, and it runs Mac OS 8.1.
