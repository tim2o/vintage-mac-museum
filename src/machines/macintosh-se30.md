An SE/30 with a Mac ROM-inator II Carbon SIMM from Big Mess o' Wires and 64 MB of RAM. Storage is a SCSIKnife SD card adapter. As sold by BMOW, the Carbon is a 4 MB flash replacement for the stock ROM SIMM that makes the SE/30 32-bit clean, which is what lets it use up to 128 MB of RAM. It also carries a bootable ROM disk with System 7.1 and utilities.

It runs a patched System 8.1, and the drive has partitions for System 7.5.3, System 6.0.8, and A/UX 3.1, so it can boot any of them.
