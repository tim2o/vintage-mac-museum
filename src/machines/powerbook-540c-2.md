A second PowerBook 540c in the collection, with a red "Ready for PowerPC upgrade" sticker next to the screen. Apple System Profiler reports a 68LC040 at 33 MHz with 36 MB of memory and System 7.6.1 Britain, and MacBench lists no FPU. Against a Quadra 630 (scored as 10.00), MacBench gives it 8.94 for the processor, 1.09 for floating point, 7.19 for disk and 7.21 for video.
