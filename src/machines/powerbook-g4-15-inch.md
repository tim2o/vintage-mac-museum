A 15-inch PowerBook G4 running Mac OS X 10.5.9 from a volume named Sorbet Leopard. The desktop also shows a volume named Tiger. System Profiler reports a 1.67 GHz PowerPC G4 with a 512 KB L2 cache and two 1 GB memory modules, and the SSD, which sits behind an IDE to SATA adapter, shows up on the ATA bus as a 59.7 GB TS64GSSD25-M. Homebrew is installed on it, and neofetch reports 26 packages.
