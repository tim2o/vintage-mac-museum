A Performa 550 that has been upgraded with an LC 575 logic board. The board has been recapped, and its original 68LC040 has been replaced with a full 68040 at 33 MHz, which has an FPU. It has 128 MB of RAM and a BlueSCSI v2 with WiFi for storage.

It can boot into Mac OS 8.1, 7.6.1, 7.5.5, or 7.1 from separate partitions.

The processor can be overclocked to 40 MHz with the LC 475 Control Strip module. Some of the benchmark screenshots may have been taken while it was overclocked.
