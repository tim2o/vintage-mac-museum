A Color Classic that has been upgraded twice. It has an LC 550 logic board, and a Sonnet Presto Plus card gives it a 33 MHz 68040 with an FPU. It has 68 MB of RAM and runs Mac OS 8.1 with Speed Doubler 8.

Apple System Profiler identifies the machine as a Performa 275.

The photos were taken over several weeks, so the lighting varies.
