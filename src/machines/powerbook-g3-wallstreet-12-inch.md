Not yet documented. This Wallstreet was a screen donor, so it pairs a 12.1-inch passive-matrix display with a 266 or 292 MHz logic board. Apple shipped both of those speeds with 14.1-inch screens; the 12.1-inch passive-matrix display came on the 233 MHz model. Which board it has will be confirmed on a closer look: a 292 MHz board would make it a first-generation Wallstreet, a 266 MHz board a later PDQ.
