A PowerBook 5300ce on a volume named PB5300ce. Apple System Profiler reports a PowerPC 603e at 117 MHz with 64 MB of memory and no external L2 cache. In MacBench 4.0 it scores 121 for the processor, 141 for floating point and 108 for disk, where a Power Macintosh 6100/60 scores 100.
