A PowerBook G3 Kanga running Mac OS 9.1 from a disk named Mac OS 9. Apple System Profiler reports a 250 MHz PowerPC G3 with a 512 KB backside L2 cache and 96 MB of built-in memory, made up of 32 MB on the logic board and a 64 MB expansion module. TattleTech reports the same processor as a PowerPC 750 at 250.43 MHz on a 50 MHz bus. The desktop also has Netscape Communicator, an Internet Explorer alias and a VNCThing alias.
